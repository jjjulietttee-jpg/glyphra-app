How to Copy Text From a Picture on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Glyphra developer

Use an OCR app. Photograph the page or choose a photo, let the app read it, then copy the text. This guide uses Glyphra, the app we make, and compares it honestly with the alternatives.

Quick answer. On iPhone, scan or pick the photo in a photo-to-text app such as Glyphra, wait a few seconds for on-device recognition, then copy part or all of the text. For a one-off copy, Apple Live Text is free and built in.

Step by step

  1. Open Glyphra and start a scanTap the scan button. On a phone with the system document camera it opens that camera; otherwise you pick photos from your library.
  2. Photograph the page or choose photosFill the frame with the page and keep the camera parallel to it. You can also pick one or several existing photos from your photo library.
  3. Wait a moment for recognitionGlyphra reads the page on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded.
  4. Copy the textOpen the document in the reader, then copy any part of it or the whole page.
  5. Keep or export it (optional)The scan is saved as a named, dated document. Export a text file or a PDF with a searchable text layer.

When another app may suit you better

If you only need to copy a few lines now and then, Apple Live Text is free and already on your iPhone. If you want a free tier, a long track record and a lower yearly price, Adobe Scan is the safer pick. Frequently asked questions

How do I copy text from a picture on my iPhone?

Use a photo-to-text app such as Glyphra, or Apple Live Text in Photos or the Camera. In Glyphra, scan or pick the photo, wait for recognition, then copy part or all of the text.

Can I copy text from a photo that is already in my library?

Yes. Choose the photo from your library when you start a scan; you can select several at once.

Which languages does it read?

By default Glyphra detects the language automatically. You can also choose recognition languages in Settings.

Does it keep the formatting?

It keeps the order of lines. On iOS 26 and later it can also detect paragraphs, lists and tables. Treat the result as text to edit, not a pixel-perfect copy.

Can I translate the copied text?

Yes, Glyphra uses Apple's on-device translation from the reader (a Pro feature).

Does it work offline?

Yes. Text recognition runs on your iPhone, so it works in airplane mode. Glyphra goes online only to handle purchases and your subscription.

Do I need an account, and are my pages uploaded?

No account is needed, and your pages are not uploaded. Documents stay in the app on your iPhone.

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on the photo. Printed text in even light reads best; hard shadows, blur and curled pages cost lines. Switch the reader to the page view to see which lines were recognized and fix the rest by hand.

Can I do this for free?
