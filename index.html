Glyphra: turn a photo of a page into text, on your iPhone

Glyphra is an iPhone OCR scanner for people who want text from printed pages, receipts, slides and handwriting without an account and without uploading anything. Recognition runs on the device, so it works in airplane mode.

Short answer. Glyphra reads a photographed page on your iPhone and gives you text to copy, search, translate and export as a text file or a searchable PDF. Scanning your own pages needs Glyphra Pro (3-day free trial). For a quick one-off copy, Apple Live Text is free and built in.

What it does

  • Reads printed text and handwriting on the device, with automatic language detection
  • Scans with the system document camera, or reads photos from your library (several at once)
  • Keeps every scan as a named, dated document and searches inside the text of all of them
  • Handles multi-page documents in order
  • Exports a text file or a PDF with a real, searchable text layer
  • Translates with Apple's on-device translation; taps through phone numbers, addresses, links and dates found in the text

Price

Scanning your own pages needs Glyphra Pro, an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99 per week or $29.99 per year, each with a 3-day free trial (US prices from the store listing; the app shows the price for your region before you subscribe).

When another app may suit you better

If you only need to copy a few lines now and then, Apple Live Text is free and already on your iPhone. If you want a free tier, a long track record and a lower yearly price, Adobe Scan is the safer pick. Glyphra is for people who scan often and want the pages kept on their own phone: an on-device archive, handwriting, searchable-PDF export and no account.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best iPhone app to turn a photo of a document into editable text?

It depends on the job. For a quick one-off copy, Apple Live Text is free and built in. For regular scanning with a private, searchable archive, Glyphra reads on the device with no account and no upload, but needs a Pro subscription. Adobe Scan is the largest alternative and has a free tier.

Does Glyphra work offline?

Yes. Recognition runs on your iPhone, so it works in airplane mode.

Does Glyphra upload my documents?

No. There is no account and no upload. The app goes online only to handle purchases and your subscription.

Does it read handwriting?

Yes. Handwriting is recognized on the device; neat writing in good light reads best.

How much does Glyphra cost?

Scanning your own pages needs Glyphra Pro, an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99 per week or $29.99 per year, each with a 3-day free trial (US prices from the store listing; the app shows the price for your region before you subscribe).

What can I export?

A plain text file, or a PDF that holds the page image with a real searchable text layer. Export is a Pro feature.

Can I scan many pages into one document?

Yes. Photograph all pages in one session; Glyphra keeps them in order as one document.
