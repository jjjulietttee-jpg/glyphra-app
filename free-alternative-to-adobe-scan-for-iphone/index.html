When another app may suit you better

If you only need to copy a few lines now and then, Apple Live Text is free and already on your iPhone. If you want a free tier, a long track record and a lower yearly price, Adobe Scan is the safer pick. Glyphra is for people who scan often and want the pages kept on their own phone: an on-device archive, handwriting, searchable-PDF export and no account.

Frequently asked questions

What is a free alternative to Adobe Scan for extracting text?

Apple Live Text is free and built into iOS for copying text from photos. Glyphra is a paid alternative focused on private, on-device scanning, without a free scanning tier.

Is Glyphra cheaper than Adobe Scan?

Not necessarily. Adobe lists Scan Plus at $4.99/month or $19.99/year on the US store; Glyphra Pro is $4.99/week or $29.99/year. Adobe Scan also has a free tier with a capped OCR allowance.

Why choose Glyphra over Adobe Scan?

If you want recognition on the device, no account, no upload and a searchable archive. If you want a free tier or a bigger ecosystem, choose Adobe Scan.

What happened to Microsoft Lens?

Microsoft retired it: support ended February 9, 2026 and new scans stopped after March 9, 2026. Scanning moved into OneDrive and the Microsoft 365 Copilot app.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Text recognition runs on your iPhone, so it works in airplane mode. Glyphra goes online only to handle purchases and your subscription.

Do I need an account, and are my pages uploaded?

No account is needed, and your pages are not uploaded. Documents stay in the app on your iPhone.

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on the photo. Printed text in even light reads best; hard shadows, blur and curled pages cost lines. Switch the reader to the page view to see which lines were recognized and fix the rest by hand.

Can I do this for free?

You can download Glyphra for free and see the recognition demo on its built-in sample page. Scanning your own pages needs Glyphra Pro, an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99 per week or $29.99 per year, each with a 3-day free trial (US store prices; the app shows your regional price before you subscribe). If you only need to copy a few lines, Apple Live Text in Photos and the Camera is free and built into iOS.
