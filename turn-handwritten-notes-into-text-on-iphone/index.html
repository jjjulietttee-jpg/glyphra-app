How to Turn Handwritten Notes Into Typed Text on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Glyphra developer

Photograph the page and let an OCR app that reads handwriting convert it to typed text. Glyphra does this on the iPhone, with no upload.

Quick answer. Scan the notes with Glyphra, wait for on-device recognition, check the result against the page, then copy or export it. Neat handwriting in even light gives the best results.

Step by step

  1. Write or place the notes flat and well litA flat page and even light matter more than resolution for handwriting.
  2. Scan the pageOpen Glyphra, tap scan and photograph the page, or pick a photo of it from your library.
  3. Let Glyphra read itHandwriting is read with the same on-device recognition as printed text. No upload.
  4. Check against the pageSwitch to the page view in the reader to see which lines were recognized, and correct what it missed.
  5. Copy or exportCopy the typed text, or export a text file or a searchable PDF.

When another app may suit you better

If you only need to copy a few lines now and then, Apple Live Text is free and already on your iPhone. If you want a free tier, a long track record and a lower yearly price, Adobe Scan is the safer pick. Glyphra is for people who scan often and want the pages kept on their own phone: an on-device archive, handwriting, searchable-PDF export and no account.

Frequently asked questions

Can an iPhone app turn handwriting into typed text?

Yes. Glyphra reads handwriting on the device and returns typed text you can copy or export. Neat, separated handwriting works best.

How accurate is handwriting recognition?

It varies with the writing. Clear print-style handwriting reads well; fast cursive and faint pencil are harder. No OCR is perfect, so review the result.

Does it work on whiteboards and sticky notes?

It reads any photographed text. Glare on whiteboards and low contrast reduce accuracy, so photograph straight on in even light.

Can I search my old handwritten notes?

Yes. Every scan you keep becomes a document, and search runs inside the text of all of them.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Text recognition runs on your iPhone, so it works in airplane mode. Glyphra goes online only to handle purchases and your subscription.

Do I need an account, and are my pages uploaded?

No account is needed, and your pages are not uploaded. Documents stay in the app on your iPhone.

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on the photo. Printed text in even light reads best; hard shadows, blur and curled pages cost lines. Switch the reader to the page view to see which lines were recognized and fix the rest by hand.

Can I do this for free?

You can download Glyphra for free and see the recognition demo on its built-in sample page. Scanning your own pages needs Glyphra Pro, an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99 per week or $29.99 per year, each with a 3-day free trial (US store prices; the app shows your regional price before you subscribe). If you only need to copy a few lines, Apple Live Text in Photos and the Camera is free and built into iOS.
