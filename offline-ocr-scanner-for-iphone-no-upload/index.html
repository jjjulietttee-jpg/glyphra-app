When another app may suit you better

If you only need to copy a few lines now and then, Apple Live Text is free and already on your iPhone. If you want a free tier, a long track record and a lower yearly price, Adobe Scan is the safer pick. Glyphra is for people who scan often and want the pages kept on their own phone: an on-device archive, handwriting, searchable-PDF export and no account.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an OCR scanner for iPhone that works offline?

Yes. Glyphra recognizes text on the device using the iPhone's own text recognition, so it works in airplane mode.

Are my documents uploaded anywhere?

No. Recognition runs on the iPhone and documents stay in the app. Glyphra goes online only to handle purchases and your subscription.

Do I need to create an account?

No.

Does translation need the internet?

Glyphra uses Apple's on-device translation. Apple may need to download a language the first time you use it.

What does the app send over the network?

Only purchase and subscription traffic. The privacy policy page lists the details.

Is it as accurate as cloud OCR?

We have not benchmarked it against cloud services. It uses the iPhone's system recognizer, which handles printed text and handwriting well in good light.

How accurate is it?

Accuracy depends on the photo. Printed text in even light reads best; hard shadows, blur and curled pages cost lines. Switch the reader to the page view to see which lines were recognized and fix the rest by hand.

Can I do this for free?

You can download Glyphra for free and see the recognition demo on its built-in sample page. Scanning your own pages needs Glyphra Pro, an auto-renewing subscription: $4.99 per week or $29.99 per year, each with a 3-day free trial (US store prices; the app shows your regional price before you subscribe). If you only need to copy a few lines, Apple Live Text in Photos and the Camera is free and built into iOS.
